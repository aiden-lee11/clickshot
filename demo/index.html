How it bolts together
PRINT

Cheeks + open cradle

Gusseted PETG cheeks in two bolted sections per side. Slotted saddles and straps leave the magazine and jam door clear.

BUY

Supported side pivots

608 bearings, short M8 pivot bolts, inner-race spacers and locknuts. A standard servo horn screws to a printed drive adapter.

BUY

Small bought turntable

A 4-inch lazy-Susan bearing under the deck supports the weight. Printed risers leave room for a coaxial yaw servo.

Overall length is source-based. Receiver width, loaded magazine clearance, mass and balance still need measuring. Local shell geometry and the mount dimensions are provisional, not print-ready CAD.

What the dimensions mean

Published reference: the licensed Nerf guide lists a 27-inch / 68.5 cm Rapidstrike. The model uses a 685 mm overall envelope, with the long stock shown. This is an approximate reference, not a Hasbro mechanical drawing.

Proposed mount: 360 × 300 × 12.7 mm base; 220 × 200 mm deck; 140 mm clear inner spacing; pitch pivots 250 mm above the deck. Print each tall cheek in two bolted sections for a typical small printer.

Measure on your blaster: width at both saddles, loaded magazine/stock sweep, mass and center of gravity. Retain jam-door access. Local shell shape and every fit-critical mount dimension remain provisional.
