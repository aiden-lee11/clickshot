What the dimensions mean

Published reference: the licensed Nerf guide lists a 27-inch / 68.5 cm Rapidstrike. The model uses a 685 mm overall envelope, with the long stock shown. This is an approximate reference, not a Hasbro mechanical drawing.

Proposed mount: 360 × 300 × 12.7 mm base; 220 × 200 mm deck; 140 mm clear inner spacing; pitch pivots 250 mm above the deck. Print each tall cheek in two bolted sections for a typical small printer.

Measure on your blaster: width at both saddles, loaded magazine/stock sweep, mass and center of gravity. Retain jam-door access. Local shell shape and every fit-critical mount dimension remain provisional.
